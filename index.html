What does a sonic branding agency do?

It designs how a brand sounds and keeps that sound consistent: the sonic logo, brand music, interface and product sounds, voice, and the guidelines that hold them together across advertising, apps, shops and events.

How much does sonic branding cost?

EightSix publishes these ranges: about €15,000 to €30,000 for a sonic logo project and €50,000 to €500,000 or more for a complete sonic identity system for a global brand. The price depends on scope, complexity and usage rights.

How long does a sonic branding project take?

A typical project takes 8 to 16 weeks from kickoff to delivery: two to three weeks of discovery and research, four to eight weeks of composition, two to three weeks of testing and refinement, and one to two weeks for guidelines and assets.

What is the difference between a sonic logo and sonic branding?

A sonic logo is a short, distinctive sound of one to three seconds, the audio equivalent of a visual logo. Sonic branding is the whole system: the logo plus brand music, interface sounds, voice and guidelines, built to work across all touchpoints for years.
