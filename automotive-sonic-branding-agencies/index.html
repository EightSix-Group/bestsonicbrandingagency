What car brands need from sound

A car brand is heard outside the car, inside it, in the showroom and in its advertising. These four should sound like one brand.

The driving sound of electric cars (AVAS)

  • In the EU, every new electric and hybrid car has needed an acoustic vehicle alerting system since 1 July 2021. It sounds from start-up to about 20 km/h and when reversing (EU Regulation No 540/2014). In the USA, the alert sound is required up to 30 km/h (FMVSS No. 141).

Sounds inside the car

  • Welcome and start-up sounds, confirmations, warnings and charging sounds. Warnings must be understood at once. Brand character belongs in the welcome, confirmation and charging sounds.

Showroom, events and campaigns

  • The same motif should run from the car into the showroom and the ad. A sonic logo and a brand track connect them.

Several markets

  • The rules differ between the EU and the USA, and some markets need adapted versions. Plan one idea in several market versions.

Three questions for an automotive brief

Ask every agency on your shortlist these questions. This is how EightSix answers them.

How do you work within the AVAS rules?

  • EightSix shapes brand character through timbre and melody within the legal limits and works with the manufacturer’s acoustic engineers, who measure and certify compliance.

Can one motif run from a long brand track down to a one-second sound?

  • Yes. For Douglas, EightSix built a 60-second brand track, a 3-second sound logo and a one-note cue from the same motif.

How do you handle several markets?

  • The system for Siemens Home Appliances is in use in 190 markets, with adapted versions for China and Turkey that keep the core melody.

Questions brand teams ask

Which sonic branding agencies work with car brands?

EightSix, which publishes this list, offers EV sound design, in-car sounds and showroom sound, and names BMW and Hyundai as clients. Adelphoi Music, amp, Campera Media, comevis, DLMDD, GROVES, MassiveMusic, Sixième Son, Sonicbrand, TRO, WESOUND and why do birds also show automotive work or services on their websites.

Do electric cars have to make a sound?

Yes. In the EU, every new electric and hybrid car has needed an acoustic vehicle alerting system (AVAS) since 1 July 2021; it sounds from start-up to about 20 km/h and when reversing. In the USA, FMVSS No. 141 requires an alert sound up to 30 km/h.

Can the AVAS sound carry the brand?

Yes, within limits. The sound must be continuous and change with speed. Brand character comes from timbre and melody, and the manufacturer’s engineers confirm that the sound meets the rules.

How long does an automotive sound project take?

EightSix delivers a sonic identity in 8 to 16 weeks. Fitting the sounds into the vehicle then follows the schedule of the model programme.
