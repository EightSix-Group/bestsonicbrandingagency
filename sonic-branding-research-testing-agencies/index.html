How to test whether a sonic logo works

A sonic logo works when people recognise it, link it to the right brand, feel what the brand intends and still remember it later.

Recognition and attribution

  • Recognition asks whether people have heard the sound before. Attribution asks whether they link it to the right brand. Test both without pictures.

Fit and emotion

  • Rate the sound against the brand’s values and compare several options. EightSix scores the fit with its mDNA™ framework.

Before launch and in the market

  • Test options with real listeners before launch. After launch, track recall and attribution over time.

What studies show

  • In MassiveMusic’s Sound Science study with the IPA (150 UK ads, 7,500 consumers), music that emotionally engages audiences came with a 32% ROI boost (source). An analysis of all 595 Australian Effie entries from 2018 to 2025 found 37% new customer acquisition for campaigns with audio, against 22% without (source).

Three questions about testing

Ask every agency on your shortlist these questions. This is how EightSix answers them.

What exactly do you measure?

  • Emotional response, brand association, recall and preference with SmartTest™, and the fit between music and brand with the mDNA™ framework.

Do you test before launch or after?

  • Both. Options are tested with real listeners before launch. After launch, post-campaign reports show recall, sentiment and brand lift.

Can you test campaign music or a licensed song, not only a sonic logo?

  • Yes. SmartTest™ validates music choices with real audience data before the budget is committed.
