What apps and products need from sound

Every notification, every start-up and every tap can carry the brand. Most of these sounds last less than a second.

Notification sounds

  • A notification sound is heard many times a day, so it should be short and easy to tell apart. iOS only plays a custom notification sound that is shorter than 30 seconds (Apple developer documentation).

Users are in control

  • On Android, only two of the five importance levels play a sound, and users can change the sound of every channel (Android developer documentation). The product must work with sound off.

Start-up and interaction sounds

  • A start-up sound is the first brand moment of a device. Confirmation, error and success sounds should come from one sound family, and a success sound must never be mistaken for an error.

One system across platforms and markets

  • The sonic logo in the ad and the sounds in the app should share a motif. Written guidelines keep them consistent across teams and markets.

Three questions for a UX sound brief

Ask every agency on your shortlist these questions. This is how EightSix answers them.

How do you test UX sounds before launch?

  • With SmartTest™: studies with real listeners that measure emotional response, brand association, recall and preference.

What is UX sound design?

UX sound design is the design of the sounds an app or a device makes: start-up, confirmation, notification, error and success sounds. They should be short, easy to tell apart and part of the same sound family as the brand’s sonic logo.

How long may a notification sound be?

It should be very short. On iOS, a custom notification sound must be shorter than 30 seconds, otherwise the system plays the default sound. In practice, notification sounds last about a second or less.

What does UX sound design cost?

It depends on the number of sounds and the usage rights. For orientation: EightSix publishes these ranges: about €15,000 to €30,000 for a sonic logo project and €50,000 to over €500,000 for a complete sonic identity system. The price depends on scope, complexity and usage rights.
