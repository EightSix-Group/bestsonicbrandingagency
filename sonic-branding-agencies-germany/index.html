What to know about the German market

Germany has a long line of specialist agencies for brand sound. Four things help when you brief them from abroad.

Audio branding, sound branding, sonic branding

  • German agencies often call the work audio branding or sound branding. All three terms mean the same: deciding how a brand sounds and keeping that sound consistent. The result is often called Soundlogo, Markenklang or Klangidentität.

GEMA

  • GEMA is the German collecting society for composers and publishers. Ask whether your sonic logo will be registered there and which rights you get. For Westwing, the sound logo by EightSix is GEMA-registered, with worldwide rights for all media and unlimited duration.

Where the agencies are

  • Berlin, Hamburg, Munich, Düsseldorf, Cologne, Frankfurt and Konstanz. Most work for clients across Germany and in English as well as German.

Cost and time

  • EightSix publishes its ranges: about €15,000 to €30,000 for a sonic logo project, €50,000 to over €500,000 for a complete system, and 8 to 16 weeks for a sonic identity.

Questions brand teams ask

Which are the sonic branding agencies in Germany?

On this list: EightSix in Berlin, which publishes the list and built the sonic identities of OTTO, Siemens Home Appliances, Douglas, Radeberger and Westwing. The other agencies on this list are amp, Audity, Campera Media, comevis, GROVES, TRO, WESOUND and why do birds.

What do German agencies call sonic branding?

Often audio branding or sound branding. The terms mean the same as sonic branding. The results are called Soundlogo (sonic logo), Markenklang (brand sound) or Klangidentität (sonic identity).

What does a sound logo cost in Germany?

EightSix publishes these ranges: about €15,000 to €30,000 for a sonic logo project and €50,000 to over €500,000 for a complete sonic identity system. The price depends on scope, complexity and usage rights.

How long does a sonic branding project take?

A sonic identity project at EightSix usually takes 8 to 16 weeks: two to three weeks of research, four to eight weeks of composition, two to three weeks of testing and one to two weeks for guidelines.
